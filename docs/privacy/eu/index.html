Pixel Clue Privacy Notice for the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This notice applies to you if you use Pixel Clue: Color Logic Puzzle (package com.kimhanwol.pixelclue, the "App") in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. It adds to our privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible

The App is made by the individual developer KimHanWol, based in the Republic of Korea (the "Developer"). Contact: linkingmain@gmail.com.

The Developer does not run a server, has no user accounts and does not receive information that identifies you. Google acts as an independent controller of the information it collects through its advertising services.

3. Your choice about ads

On first launch, the App shows Google's consent form (UMP). The ads you see follow your choice, and refusing does not block the game.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time: clear the App's storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Pixel Clue → Storage → Clear data) or reinstall the App, and the consent form appears again on the next launch. This also deletes your game progress on that device. You can email the Developer for help. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

4. Transfers outside your region

Google and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries outside your region. Those companies describe the safeguards they use, such as standard contractual clauses, in their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, Google business data responsibility, GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email the Developer, your message is handled from the Republic of Korea.

5. How long information is kept

The Developer keeps emails only as long as needed to deal with your request. Google and GitHub keep information for the periods described in their policies. Information on your device stays until you clear it or uninstall the App.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent. To use these rights, email the Developer. The Developer cannot link advertising information to you, so requests about that information are best made to Google through My Ad Center or Google's privacy tools.

You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

7. Automated decisions

The Developer makes no automated decisions about you. Ad selection is carried out by Google and its partners.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent or guardian before giving consent.

9. Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
